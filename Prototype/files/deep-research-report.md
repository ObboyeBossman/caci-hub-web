# Official Colors  
Christ Apostolic Church International (Ghana) uses **red, white, and blue** as its primary colors【4†L74-L76】.  These appear on the church’s flag and logo.  According to church materials, red represents Christ’s redeeming blood, white stands for holiness/righteousness, and blue for divine hope or judgment【4†L74-L76】【13†L39-L43】.  For app design, the approximate brand color values are:

- **Red** – #C60026 (approximately)  
- **White** – #FFFFFF  
- **Blue** – #004BA0 (approximately)  

These hex codes are based on the official logo/flag imagery. (Official sources describe the colors but do not publish exact codes【4†L74-L76】【13†L39-L43】; the above values are close matches for design use.)
